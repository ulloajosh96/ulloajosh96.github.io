Conceptual Liquid Rocket Engine Design & Performance Analysis

Designed a liquid rocket engine end to end from a stated thrust requirement, running thermodynamic and compressible flow trade studies to size the chamber, injector, feed system, and nozzle, then validating the design in CFD.

← All projects

Overview

Starting from a stated thrust requirement, I worked through the thermodynamic and compressible flow relationships that govern rocket engine performance to set chamber pressure, mixture ratio, and nozzle expansion ratio. Those numbers then drove the physical sizing: the combustion chamber, an injector concept, the propellant feed system, and a converging diverging nozzle, treated as a single coupled system rather than independent parts.

Once the design was sized, I built a parametric 3D CAD assembly and CFD models specifically to check my own analytical predictions for nozzle flow and thermal loads against simulation, rather than trusting the hand calculations on their own.
