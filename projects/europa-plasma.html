Large-Scale Plasma Simulation Data Analysis

Processed and analyzed 3D hybrid plasma simulation datasets exceeding 200 million data points to characterize Europa's magnetosphere interactions and magnetic field perturbations, in support of an ongoing publication.

← All projects

Overview

This project worked with 3D hybrid plasma simulation datasets describing the interaction between Europa and Jupiter's surrounding magnetospheric plasma, in support of research into Europa's induced magnetic field and what it implies about a subsurface ocean. A single simulation cube contained on the order of 200 million spatial grid points, too large to treat casually as an ordinary in-memory array.

I built memory-optimized Python workflows, chunked processing and memory-mapped arrays, to make datasets that size tractable, then developed automated masking, thresholding, and feature-extraction pipelines to reduce raw simulation output into scientifically meaningful subsets. The results are supporting an ongoing publication.
