Neutron Star Equation-of-State Simulation

Implemented a fourth order Runge Kutta solver for the coupled relativistic differential equations governing neutron star structure, testing three published equations of state.

← All projects

Overview

Neutron stars are dense enough that ordinary Newtonian hydrostatics doesn't apply, so this project used a relativistic stellar-structure formulation instead, a system of coupled differential equations describing how mass and pressure change with radius. I implemented a fourth-order Runge-Kutta integrator to solve them, handling the singular behavior near the stellar center before transitioning to the general integration equations.

Sweeping across central density conditions and three published nuclear equations of state let me build out full mass-radius relationships for each model and compare how sensitive the predicted neutron star properties are to the underlying nuclear physics assumptions.
