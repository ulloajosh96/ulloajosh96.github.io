ICEMAN — Subglacial Plume Dynamics Apparatus

Designed 3D CAD models for a lab scale experimental apparatus, translating experimental requirements into a physical design for a multidisciplinary research team.

← All projects

Overview

For a research project studying subglacial plume dynamics, I designed 3D CAD models for a lab-scale experimental apparatus, including freshwater inflow channels and tank modifications, translating what the experiment actually needed into a design that would hold up under real lab conditions.

I synthesized findings from prior experimental studies to inform design decisions, and briefed technical progress weekly to a multidisciplinary research team to keep the design aligned with the project's objectives.
